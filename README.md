## ✨ Features & Highlights

- **36+ Curated Destinations**: Complete guide across **CP / Central Delhi, Rajouri Garden & Punjabi Bagh, Pacific Mall (Tagore Garden), Mandi House, Vegas Mall (Dwarka), and Paschim Vihar Local**.
- **Tested Target Budgets**: Clear budget estimates for 2 people (Targeting ₹1,000 ceiling with ₹200–300 buffer recommendations).
- **Direct Action Links**:
  - 📍 **Google Maps**: One-tap direct location navigation.
  - 🍽️ **Zomato Dining**: Instant link to check Zomato dining discounts & reviews.
  - 🛵 **Swiggy Dineout**: Instant link to Swiggy Dineout offers.
  - 🌐 **Official Website**: Direct links to official cafe/mall portals.
- **10-Second Quick Decision Matrix**: Instant top recommendations for *Best Overall Day Date*, *Best Evening Date*, *Best ₹1,000 Dinner*, *Quiet & Classy Vibe*, *Proper CP Dinner*, and *Best Coffee Stop*.
- **4 Ready-Made Date Plans**:
  - **Plan A — Pacific Mall**: Day Date (12:00 PM – 4:30 PM)
  - **Plan B — Cine Tree + CP**: Evening Date (4:30 PM – 8:15 PM)
  - **Plan C — Kartoon Cafe + Rajouri**: Youthful Evening (5:00 PM – 8:15 PM)
  - **Plan D — Triveni Terrace**: Quiet Artsy Date (4:00 PM – 7:45 PM)
- **Instagram Curator Hub & QR Modal (`@iavinaxh`)**: Interactive popup featuring Avinash's Instagram QR code and direct follow link.
- **Delhi Metro Route Cheat Sheet**: Station interchanges, travel durations, and live DMRC advisory notes starting from Paschim Vihar West station.
- **Wishlist Bucket List**: Save your favorite date spots directly to browser local storage.
