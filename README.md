## ✨ Features & Highlights

- **36+ Curated Destinations**: A growing guide across **CP / Central Delhi, Rajouri Garden & Punjabi Bagh, Pacific Mall (Tagore Garden), Mandi House, Vegas Mall (Dwarka), and West Delhi**.
- **Budget-first planning**: Clear estimates for 2 people, with practical room for tax, an extra drink, dessert or last-mile travel.
- **Direct Action Links**:
  - 📍 **Google Maps**: One-tap location/navigation links.
  - 🍽️ **Zomato**: Restaurant and dining pages where available.
  - 🛵 **Swiggy Dineout**: Shown only when a verified Dineout destination is available in the cafe data.
  - 🌐 **Website**: Official cafe or venue website where available.
- **10-Second Quick Decision Matrix**: Instant recommendations for *Best Overall Day Date*, *Best Evening Date*, *Best ₹1,000 Dinner*, *Quiet & Classy Vibe*, *Proper CP Dinner*, and *Best Coffee Stop*.
- **4 Ready-Made Date Plans**:
  - **Plan A — Pacific Mall**: Day Date (12:00 PM – 4:30 PM)
  - **Plan B — Cine Tree + CP**: Evening Date (4:30 PM – 8:15 PM)
  - **Plan C — Kartoon Cafe + Rajouri**: Youthful Evening (5:00 PM – 8:15 PM)
  - **Plan D — Triveni Terrace**: Quiet Artsy Date (4:00 PM – 7:45 PM)
- **Delhi Metro Route Cheat Sheet**: Useful destination/interchange guidance designed for visitors coming from different parts of Delhi NCR.
- **Wishlist Bucket List**: Save your favorite date spots directly to browser local storage.
- **Instagram Curator Hub & QR Modal (`@iavinaxh`)**: Interactive popup featuring the curator's Instagram QR code and follow link.
